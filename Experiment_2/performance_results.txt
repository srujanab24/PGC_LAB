OBSERVATIONS

1. Parallel execution reduces the execution time compared with sequential execution.

2. For Pthreads, the lowest measured execution time was obtained with 4 threads.

3. For OpenMP, the lowest measured execution time was obtained with 4 threads.

4. Increasing the number of threads beyond 4 did not provide further performance improvement in these experiments.

5. Efficiency decreases as the number of threads increases because the speedup is not proportional to the number of threads.

6. The 1-thread efficiency slightly exceeds 100% due to normal execution-time measurement variation.

CONCLUSION

The experiment demonstrates thread creation, work distribution, synchronization, race-condition handling, and performance measurement using Pthreads and OpenMP. The results show that parallel execution can reduce computation time, while excessive thread counts introduce overhead and reduce efficiency.
